Covenant Rent Rescue Program

“Relief for today. Stewardship for tomorrow.”

The Covenant Rent Rescue Program provides one month of rent assistance ($1,500–$3,000) to families experiencing temporary financial hardship such as job loss, medical emergencies, or unexpected crises.

A Structured Reset

Unlike traditional aid programs, this is not just financial relief — it is a structured reset. The goal is not dependency, but restoration — helping families regain stability while learning sustainable financial discipline. Every recipient must complete:

  • A mandatory financial planning session
  • A detailed income vs. expense audit
  • Creation of a survival budget
  • A 90-day stabilization plan
  • Biblical stewardship guidance
  • A follow-up accountability check

Program Safeguards

  • Direct Payments

    Assistance is paid directly to landlords, never to the individual.

  • Verified Need

    Full documentation and proof of income/hardship are strictly required.

  • One-Time Aid

    No repeat assistance permitted within a 12-month period.

  • Mandatory Counseling

    Participation in our financial counseling sessions is non-negotiable.
